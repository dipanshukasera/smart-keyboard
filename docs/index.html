Privacy

Learning stays on your phone

  • What you type is learned on the device. Suggestions, next words and your word list are never uploaded.
  • Some features need the server. Hindi from English letters, GIF search and translation send only the word or phrase in that box.
  • Passwords and incognito are left alone. Nothing is learned, suggested or sent from them.
